## What is actually usable

Only SCB PSU was verified to publish a reconstructable national
previous-vote × current-intention table before the 2018 and 2022 elections.
The strict waves are both from May, roughly three months before election day.
Commercial pollsters commonly collect previous vote for weighting, but their
public reports expose only national marginals or selected narrative flows.

The 2022 API table contains point estimates, 95% margins and unweighted row
bases. Some small transition estimates are suppressed. They are not observed
zeros and must remain explicitly marked. Respondent-level weights and raw cell
counts are not public.

The original June 2018 publication is used for the strict 2018 test. It was
recovered through the Kungliga biblioteket URN resolver, pinned by SHA-256, and
Table 21 is parsed directly from PDF page 99. SCB recalculated historical PSU
estimates in 2020, so current PxWeb values for 2018M05 are not substituted.

## Cutoff availability

No full public transition matrix was verified at T-30, T-14, T-7, T-3 or T-1
for either 2018 or 2022. Those cutoffs are unavailable for T0. Late marginal
polls may later update the national state, but they cannot manufacture a
previous-party × current-party matrix.

## Demographic and poststratification inventory

No open official source supplies
`district × previous_vote × age × sex × education`. Valmyndigheten has
district-level eligible-voter age × sex tables. SCB has municipality/region
age × sex × education joints and DeSO age × sex and education as separate
tables. Combining these with previous vote would require an explicitly
synthetic P0/P1/P2 population. That work is deferred until T0 earns it.

## Provenance rule

VALU and election-study matrices are retained only as
`RETROSPECTIVE_DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY`. They must never enter a strict pre-election
backtest because they use election-day responses, post-election publication or
calibration to the realized target result.
